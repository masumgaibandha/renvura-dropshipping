# Hero slide images — source reference

Not served to the browser (Next.js only serves files it's asked for by path), kept here purely
for traceability per the Phase 24 hero-imagery brief. These are real product/lifestyle photos
sourced from the supplier's own wider marketplace catalog (SelfShop, `selfshop.com.bd`) via its
authenticated dropshipper browsing session — read-only discovery, no purchase/cart/order actions
were ever taken. Each was hand-picked for a genuinely premium, clutter-free composition (no
marketplace watermark, no discount sticker, no competitor branding dominating the frame) rather
than the busier AliExpress-style marketing photography most of the already-ingested Renvura
catalog images carry. No wholesale price or other supplier-internal data is embedded in any of
these files — they are plain product/lifestyle photographs.

**Rejected during selection** (documented for the record, not used): an "OLEVS" chronograph watch
photo (a prominent branded logo box dominated one corner — more than the brief's "small
unavoidable" allowance); an "S10 MAX Smart Watch" listing whose photo is a near-exact match to
Apple's own Apple Watch marketing photography for what reads as an unauthorized clone product —
excluded under the brief's "no counterfeit/replica-looking products" rule. Most `Electronic
Accessories`/`TV & Home Appliances` listings on SelfShop carry heavy baked-in marketing
badges/competitor branding and were excluded wholesale for the same reason `HeroBanner`'s original
single campaign image was replaced in the first place.
